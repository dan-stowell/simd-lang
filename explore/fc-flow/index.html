READ THIS AS A MODEL, NOT A BENCHMARK. Particle counts, delays and relative speeds are illustrative; they do not encode measured phase timings. Stock Go also compiles packages in parallel and caches builds; this depicts a cold build. fc still has dependency constraints, allocations, source reads and a final executable write. Not every package is simultaneously ready.

Local evidence: driver/fc/build.go (in-process linking and assembly), PLAN.md and JOURNAL.md (declarations/body scheduling, direct emission). The reported November 15 morning MinIO cold result was 2.4–2.7s on 8 cores with huge pages; later correctness fixes still need a quiet rerun. This illustration does not claim that fc produces equally optimized machine code, or that its entire speedup comes from data movement.
